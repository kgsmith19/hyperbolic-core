# Creative Standard · v1.0

## Purpose
Create distinctive, studio-grade visual explanations, diagrams, presentations, and interactive stories for personal use. Bold editorial art direction, meaningful cinematic movement, and original storytelling are preferred over generic SaaS templates.

## Five non-negotiables
1. **Typography leads:** expressive hierarchy, deliberate type pairing, clear rhythm, excellent readability.
2. **Composition over cards:** varied editorial grids, asymmetry where useful, intentional negative space, precise alignment.
3. **One strong idea per scene:** meaningful visual metaphor, selective color, purposeful imagery, no gratuitous decoration.
4. **Motion tells the story:** choreograph reveals, transitions, and transformations to explain causality or guide attention; avoid perpetual pulsing and parallax for its own sake.
5. **Critique and refine:** compare multiple directions, inspect real screenshots, test interactions, and revise weak work.

## Avoid by default
- Neon glows, generic purple gradients, glassmorphism, repeated rounded cards.
- Decorative icons, invented statistics, meaningless charts, generic AI copy.
- Constant animations, scroll hijacking, distracting camera moves.
- Copying distinctive visual identities or using assets without permission.

## Quality gates
- **Editorial:** clear audience, thesis, accurate content, compelling narrative arc.
- **Visual:** strong hierarchy, intentional typography, consistent spacing and color semantics, no template feel.
- **Interaction:** keyboard access, visible focus, touch support, understandable controls and states.
- **Motion:** readable pacing, reduced-motion alternative, no essential information only in animation.
- **Technical:** responsive layouts, measured performance, cross-browser checks, no console errors.
- **Legal:** verify license, attribution and distribution rights for every dependency and asset.

## Process
Brief → references and art direction → storyboard → design system → prototype → critique → accessibility/performance review → publish.

## Rule of judgment
Choose the simplest technique that produces the intended feeling and understanding. A static diagram is preferable to a flashy animation when it communicates better.
