03 / Cinematic chapter transitions

Make
space for
wonder.

A restrained film-like narrative with pinned scenes, a changing palette, and scroll-responsive composition.

ACT I / THE UNKNOWN

First, we notice
the question.

A story starts when something refuses to be ignored.

ACT II / THE SHIFT

Then the pieces
find their place.

Relationships appear; the scene changes with understanding.

ACT III / THE ANSWER

What remains
is meaning.

Movement should resolve into something the reader can keep.

Designed to explain, not decorate.
